THE SECRET LIFE OF WALTER MITTY

Title : The Secret Life of Walter Mitty
Author : James Thurber
Genre : Short Story, Comedy / Satire
Published : 1939
Theme : Imagination, escape from reality, ordinary life
Characters : Walter Mitty, Mrs. Mitty

Walter Mitty is a simple, middle-aged man who lives an ordinary life with his wife, Mrs. Mitty. He often feels that his life is boring and that his wife controls many aspects of his daily life. To escape from reality, he creates exciting adventures in his imagination.

One day, while driving with his wife, Walter begins to daydream. He imagines himself as a brave Navy commander flying a dangerous aircraft through a storm. His imagination is interrupted when his wife tells him to drive carefully.

Later, Walter imagines himself as a famous doctor performing a difficult operation and saving a patient's life. Again, his fantasy ends when someone speaks to him.

He then imagines himself as a brave military officer facing danger without fear. In another daydream, he becomes a skilled pilot involved in an exciting mission.

Throughout the day, Walter moves between the real world and his imaginary world. In reality, he is forgetful, nervous, and often criticized by his wife. In his imagination, however, he is confident, intelligent, brave, and powerful.

At the end of the story, Walter waits outside while his wife shops. He imagines himself standing bravely before a firing squad, facing death without fear.

The story ends with Walter enjoying his imaginary heroic identity, even though his real life is ordinary.

🌟 Moral : Dream big, but don’t forget to live in the real world.
